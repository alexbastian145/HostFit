How HostFit works

HostFit uses fixed, readable rules rather than machine learning, so every recommendation can be explained.

1. Estimate what you need

Your use case sets a workload profile. From your monthly visits we estimate vCPU, RAM and transfer.

Transfer includes 25% headroom. Database RAM adds about 10% of data size. Shared hosting is excluded for backends and databases, and for sites above a visit ceiling.

2. Filter out plans that cannot work

A plan is dropped if it is over budget, or has too little storage, bandwidth, vCPU or RAM, or is an unsuitable type. Plans that fail only on budget are shown separately as "slightly over budget".

3. Score what is left (0–100)

  • Resource fit – up to 35 points

Resource fit rewards plans with 1.2×–2× headroom and penalises over-provisioning. Price rewards plans that are cheap relative to your budget and to the cheapest alternative.

Where prices come from

A scheduled crawler reads public provider APIs and pricing pages every few hours and records every change. Plans carry a badge: Live fetched automatically, Verified confirmed by an administrator, Estimate starter data not yet checked, and Stale not re-checked recently. Dollar prices are converted to INR at a configured rate. Always confirm the final price (taxes, renewal pricing, billing term) on the provider's site.
